# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want rust-anything to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that make the project better:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including in disagreement.
- Giving and taking constructive criticism gracefully. Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Assuming good intent, and asking when something is unclear.
- Owning mistakes and learning from them.

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@erkamyaman](https://github.com/erkamyaman),
through GitHub. For something you'd rather not post in public, use
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/erkamyaman/rust-anything/security/advisories/new) and
say it's a conduct report. Every report is reviewed, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that break this code, and may ban, temporarily or permanently, anyone whose behavior is harmful.
